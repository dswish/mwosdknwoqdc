Hello, I’m

Dean Baker

My name is Dean Baker. I am 19 years old and live in Havertown, Pennsylvania. I currently attend Drexel University, where I am interested in studying Computer Science.

As a kid, I loved exploring different computer applications and websites. As I grew up, I became especially interested in learning more about artificial intelligence and how widely it is now used. I am taking this course to gain valuable experience that I can use after college as I pursue a career in the technology industry.

Grammarly was the first AI-powered tool I remember using. Its ability to support better grammar and writing felt revolutionary. At the same time, my biggest concern about AI is that people may use it in harmful ways, create disturbing content, or use it to automate jobs without considering the impact on people and communities.

I am most excited to identify and discuss ethical issues associated with AI and machine learning and their societal implications. My goal is to better understand how we can use generative AI responsibly and correctly. Outside of technology, I am also a very good cook and enjoy creating all kinds of dishes, even when they are simple.
